I graduated in Computer Science with a focus on Artificial Intelligence, and lately I've been pushing hard into full-stack development. Somewhere in that transition I realised I was learning a lot but keeping none of it. This blog fixes that.

## Why write in public

Writing forces clarity. If I can't explain a concept in plain words, I don't really understand it yet. Publishing it raises the stakes just enough to make me get it right.

> The best way to learn is to teach. The second best is to write it down where someone might read it.

## What to expect here

- Notes on full-stack work — Node.js, Express, databases, APIs
- AI and ML experiments — agents, RAG, vector search, local models
- Build logs from my side projects
- Things that broke and how I fixed them
